<Aside type="tip" title="Idea">  Set up and manage webhooks in YourGPT </Aside>

**YourGPT Webhooks** allow you to receive real-time notifications about events happening within your AI chatbot integration.


## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Setting up Webhooks</div>

- Go to your YourGPT integration settings.
- Select **`Webhook`** and click on **`Manage`**.
<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 ">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webhook/webhook.png"
  alt=" Webhook Setup"
  width="100%"
  height="100%"
  style={{ width: "100%" }}
  className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>
- Click on **`Add New Webhook`**.
<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 ">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webhook/new-webhook.png"
  alt=" Add new Webhook"
  width="100%"
  height="100%"
  style={{ width: "100%" }}
  className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>
- Click on **`Edit`** Add the webhook URL and then 'Select the events you want to subscribe to'.
<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webhook/webhook event.png"
  alt=" Add Webhook Events"
  width="100%"
  height="100%"
  style={{ width: "100%" }}
  className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>


- To complete the setup click on **`Create`**.

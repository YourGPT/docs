## <span  className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Installation</span>
Integrating YourGPT Chatbot with Carrd is a simple process that ensures seamless interaction with your website. Follow these steps:

<Steps>
1. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Sign in to Carrd:</div>
    - Log in to your Carrd account.

2. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Access Site Editor:</div>
   - Open the site editor for the specific Carrd website where you want to integrate the chatbot.

3. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Add an Element:</div>
    - Click on the **`Add an element`** (Plus icon) for your website.

4. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Select Embed:</div>
    - Scroll down and choose the **`Embed`** option within the **`Add an element`** section. This allows you to add custom scripts or HTML code.

5. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">YourGPT Chatbot Integration:</div>

   - Visit the YourGPT Chatbot integration page. Copy the **`code snippet`** from the integrations section at the top.

6. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Paste the Code:</div>
   - Return to the Carrd website editor and paste the copied Chatbot code into the embed area meant for custom scripts or HTML.

7. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Save Changes:</div>
    - Ensure to save the modifications made in the **`Custom Code`** section within Carrd.

</Steps>

Congratulations! YourGPT Chatbot is now successfully integrated into your Carrd website.

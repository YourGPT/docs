## Installation

Integrating YourGPT Chatbot with Bubble is a simple process that ensures seamless interaction with your website. Follow these steps:

### Install Chatbot Plugin

1. Access your Bubble dashboard and navigate to the **```Plugins```** section.
2. Click on **```Add New```** to explore available plugins.
3. In the search bar, look for **```YourGPT Chatbot```**.
4. Select **```YourGPT Chatbot```** from the search results and proceed to install it.

### Post-Installation Configuration

1. After successful installation, you'll notice a prompt guiding you to set up the YourGPT Chatbot for the first time.
2. Visit [**```YourGPT Chatbot```**](https://yourgpt.ai/chatbot) to create your customised chatbot tailored to your website's needs.
3. Within the 'Integration' section of YourGPT Chatbot's dashboard, copy your unique Widget Id and Chatbot Id assigned specifically to your chatbot.
4. Return to your Bubble dashboard and find the installed YourGPT Chatbot plugin.
5. Paste the acquired Widget Id and Chatbot Id into the designated fields within the plugin settings on your Bubble dashboard.
6. Save the changes, and congratulations! Your Chatbot is now fully integrated and ready to engage users on your Bubble website.

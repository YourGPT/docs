## Installation

Integrating YourGPT Chatbot with your Webflow site is a straightforward process that improves user interaction. Follow these steps:


### Installation via Webflow Marketplace

1. Log in to your Webflow account.
2. Visit [**```YourGPT Chatbot on Webflow Marketplace```**](https://webflow.com/apps/detail/yourgpt-chatbot).

3. Click on **```Install```** to add the YourGPT Chatbot app to your Webflow account.

<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webflow/image1.png"
  alt="Install"
/>
  
4. After installation, select the project within Webflow where you wish to add the chatbot.

<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webflow/image2.png"
  alt="Select the chatbot"
/>

5. **```Authorize```** the YourGPT Chatbot to add Chatbot to the selected project.

<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/webflow/image4.png"
  alt="Authorize"
/>

6. Once authorized, the YourGPT Chatbot will be added to your Webflow project.

Congratulations! YourGPT Chatbot is now integrated into your Webflow project.

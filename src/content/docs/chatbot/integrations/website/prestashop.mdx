## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'> Step-by-Step Integration Guide </div>

Integrating YourGPT with your PrestaShop store is a seamless process that enhances customer interaction and boosts conversions. Follow these simple steps to get started:

<Steps>
1. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl">Download the YourGPT PrestaShop Module</div>
   - Download the latest **YourGPT module (ZIP file)** from the [github repo](https://github.com/yourgpt-ai/yourgpt-prestashop)

2. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl">Log in to Your PrestaShop Admin</div>
   - Go to your PrestaShop **Admin Panel**
   - In the left menu, navigate to **Modules → Module Manager**
   
   <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 mt-4">
     <Image src="/assets/chatbot/prestashop/prestashop1.png" alt="PrestaShop Admin Panel" 
            width="100%" height="100%" style={{ width: "100%" }} 
            className="rounded-md object-contain shadow-lg" />
   </div>

3. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl">Upload the YourGPT Module</div>
   - Click the **"Upload a Module"** button (top-right)
   - Upload the `yourgptchatbot.zip` file you downloaded earlier
   - Wait for the module to install
   - Once installed, click **"Configure"**
   
   <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 mt-4">
     <Image src="/assets/chatbot/prestashop/prestashop2.png" alt="Upload YourGPT Module" 
            width="100%" height="100%" style={{ width: "100%" }} 
            className="rounded-md object-contain shadow-lg" />
   </div>

4. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl">Add Your Widget UID</div>
   - Head to your YourGPT Dashboard and copy your **Widget UID**
   - Paste the **Widget UID** into the input field inside the YourGPT module settings
   - Click **"Save"**
   
   <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 mt-4">
     <Image src="/assets/chatbot/prestashop/prestashop3.png" alt="Add Widget UID" 
            width="100%" height="100%" style={{ width: "100%" }} 
            className="rounded-md object-contain shadow-lg" />
   </div>

</Steps>

✅ That's it! The YourGPT chatbot will now appear on your PrestaShop store.

<Aside type="note">
  <div className="font-bold">Pro Tip:</div> Test your chatbot on multiple devices (desktop, tablet, mobile) to ensure responsive behavior across all screen sizes.
</Aside>

## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'> How to Manage or Update the YourGPT Module </div>

1. Go to **Modules → Module Manager**
2. Search for **YourGPT**
3. From here, you can **configure**, **disable**, or **uninstall** the module anytime

## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Troubleshooting </div>

If you encounter issues with your YourGPT chatbot on PrestaShop, try these troubleshooting steps:

#### Chatbot Not Appearing

* Clear your browser cache and reload the page
* Verify the module is enabled in Module Manager
* Check that your Widget UID is correctly entered
* Ensure your PrestaShop theme is compatible with the module
* Temporarily disable other chat or popup modules that might conflict

#### Performance Issues

* Optimize your chatbot's knowledge base size
* Check your PrestaShop store's overall performance
* Consider upgrading your hosting if your store experiences slowdowns
* Verify that your PrestaShop version is compatible with the YourGPT module

## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'> Installation </div>

Integrating YourGPT Chatbot with your HTML website. Follow these simple steps:

1. **Access Your HTML Website:** Open your HTML website in the editor of your choice.

2. **Copy the Widget Code:** <div><p>Visit the <a href="https://yourgpt.ai/chatbot" target="_blank" className='no-underline'><strong>YourGPT Chatbot</strong></a> -> <strong>Access your chatbot</strong> -> Go to the Integration page and <strong>copy the code</strong> located in the script section at the top of your screen.</p></div>

4. **Save Changes:** Ensure to save the changes made to your HTML code editor.

 Connecting Your AI Chatbot with Instagram
</Note> 

## <span  className="nx-text-3xl">Prerequisites</span>

Before integrating Instagram with YourGPT Chatbot, ensure you have the following prerequisites in place:

- **[```Meta Developer App```](https://developers.facebook.com/apps)** to manage connections between Instagram and YourGPT Chatbot, including webhooks and access tokens.
- Administrator or Developer roles for a **[```Facebook Page```](https://www.facebook.com/)** since Instagram accounts need to be linked to a Facebook page for using the Facebook Graph API.
- An **[```Instagram Professional Account```](https://www.instagram.com/)** is required to access features specific to professional accounts.

## <span  className="nx-text-3xl">Setting up Instagram Integration</span>

1. Go to the **[```Integration Section```](https://chatbot.yourgpt.ai/)** in YourGPT Chatbot and Connect the Instagram integration if not already installed.
2. Click **```Connect Instagram```**. Proceed to your chatbot settings.
3. Add the following details in the Instagram section:

- **App ID:** Your YourGPT app's identification, created at Meta Developer platform.
- **App Secret:** The secret key of your YourGPT app.
- **Page ID:** The ID of your Facebook page.
- **Access Token:** The access token.

## <span  className="nx-text-3xl">Setting up Instagram</span>

### <span  className="nx-text-xl">Create a Facebook Page</span>

- If you don't have a Facebook page, **[```create one```](https://www.facebook.com/pages/create)**. Ensure you have an administrator or developer role to link your chatbot.

### <span  className="nx-text-xl">Create a YourGPT Chatbot App</span>

1. Log in to **[```Facebook for Developers```](https://developers.facebook.com/)**.
2. Select My Apps and **```create a new app```**.
3. Choose Business as the App Type.
4. Assign a name to your app.

### <span  className="nx-text-xl">App ID and Secret</span>

1. In Settings, under Basic, find the **```App ID```** and **```App Secret```**.
2. Click Show in the App Secret text box. Copy both for channel configuration.

### <span  className="nx-text-xl">Page ID and Access Token</span>

1. In Messenger settings, under Instagram Settings, click **```Add or Remove Pages```**.
2. Add your Facebook page and copy the number under your page name for **```Page ID```**.
3. Click **```Generate Token```** and copy for **```Access Token```**.


## <span  className="nx-text-xl">Submit your App for Meta Review</span>

During Development Mode, your bot is accessible to admins, developers, and testers only. After approval and public release, it becomes available to the general public.

Learn more about the **[```App Review process```](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/review)**. For troubleshooting, follow **[```this documentation```](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-review/support/rejection-guides/instagram)** if your app got rejection.

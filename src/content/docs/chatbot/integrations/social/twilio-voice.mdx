To set up Twilio Voice integration, follow these steps:
<Steps>
 1. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl"> Obtain Twilio Credentials </div>
      - Log into your Twilio account.
      - Go to the "**Develop**" tab to find your Account SID and Auth Token.
     <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
        <Image 
          src="/assets/chatbot/Twilio-voice/Twilio-1.png"
          alt="Twilio Credentials" 
          width="100%"
                                      height="100%"
                                        style={{ width: "100%" }}
                                        className="rounded-md" 
        />
      </div>

  2. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl"> Get Phone Number SID </div>
      - Go to "**View All Numbers**."
      - Select your number, go to "**Properties**," and copy your Phone Number SID.
      
  3. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl"> Configure Integration </div>
                              
          - Add the following fields:
            - **Account SID**: Your Twilio account SID.
            - **AuthToken**: Your Twilio Auth Token.
            - **Voice Phone SID**: The SID of your Twilio voice number.
            - **Welcome Message**: Initial greeting message (e.g., "Hello, how can we assist you today?").
            - **Error Message**: Message played when no speech is detected.
            - **Voice Language**: Language used during the call.
            - **Voice Type**: Voice model used (e.g., Polly.Aditi).
            - **Speech Timeout**: Duration to wait for a user's speech response.
          <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
            <Image 
              src="/assets/chatbot/Twilio-voice/Twilio-2.png"
              alt="Integration Configuration" 
              width="100%"
                                          height="100%"
                                            style={{ width: "100%" }}
                                            className="rounded-md" 
            />
          </div>                          

  4. <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-lg md:text-xl"> Set Webhook Endpoint </div>
  
        - Obtain your webhook endpoint.
        - Add this webhook to your phone number in the Voice configuration:
           - Go to "**Develop**" → "**View All Numbers**" → Select your number → "**Configure**."
        <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
            <Image 
              src="/assets/chatbot/Twilio-voice/Twilio-3.png"
              alt="Webhook Configuration" 
              width="100%"
                                            height="100%"
                                              style={{ width: "100%" }}
                                              className="rounded-md" 
            />
          </div>



</Steps>

## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'> Setting up the Discord Integration </div>
To integrate Discord with your application, follow these steps:
### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Obtain Application Credentials</div>

- Copy the **```Application ID```**.
- Copy the **```Public Key```**.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/Discord/discord-2.png"
  alt="Application Credentials"
  width="100%"
             height="100%"
               style={{ width: "100%" }}
               className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Create a Bot and Generate Token</div>

- Create a **```Bot```** within your application.
- Generate a **```Token```** for the bot and copy it.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/Discord/discord-3.png"
  alt="Create Bot and Token"
  width="100%"
             height="100%"
               style={{ width: "100%" }}
               className="rounded-md"
/>
</div>

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Enable the Bot</div>

- Enable the bot using the copied credentials within the Discord Developer Portal.
<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 md:px-16">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/Discord/discord-4.png"
  alt="Enable Bot"
  width="100%"
             height="100%"
               style={{ width: "100%" }}
               className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Obtain Webhook</div>

- Copy the **```Webhook```** URL for your bot.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4 md:px-16">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/Discord/discord-5.png"
  alt="Obtain Webhook"
   width="100%"
             height="100%"
               style={{ width: "100%" }}
               className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Integration Process</div>

- Paste the copied **```Webhook```** URL into your application.
- Successful integration means the webhook is added; otherwise, update the integration credentials with the correct data.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
<Image 
  src="/assets/chatbot/Discord/discord-6.png"
  alt="Integration Process"
  width="100%"
             height="100%"
               style={{ width: "100%" }}
               className="rounded-md object-contain"
/>
</div>

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Add Permissions</div>

- Go to OAuth 2 and go to Bot permissions.
- Grant the Bot necessary text permissions.
- Copy the generate URL and paste it to your browser and Add the bot to your server.


### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Test the Integration</div>
To initiate a conversation, you must use chat slash commands. For instance, you can type **```/ask```**.

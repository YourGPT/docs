## Training Integration Guide for YourGPT


Your AI becomes more smarter and efficient when you train it with the right data. YourGPT provides you with the flexibility to train your AI with your own data. You can train your AI with the data you have collected from your customers, or you can use the data from the internet.

## <span  className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>How To Train Using Your Sources</span>

1. Login to [YourGPT Dashboard](https://chatbot.yourgpt.ai/).

2. Go to the `Training` Section in the YourGPT Dashboard.

3. Choose the training option you want to use. You can choose from the multiple knowledge source options available on platform.

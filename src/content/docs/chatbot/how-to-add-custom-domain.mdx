To add a custom domain for your widget, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Widget Section**
    - Log in to your account and go to the **Widget** section.

   ![branding-widget](/assets/chatbot/custom-domain/custom-domain.png)

2. **Access Branding Settings**
    - Click on the **Branding** option in the menu.
3. **Manage Widget Domain**
    - Select **Manage Widget Domain** from the available options.

  ![add-custom-domain](/assets/chatbot/custom-domain/add-domain-name.png)
  
4. **Add Your Domain**
    - Enter your desired domain name in the provided field.
5. **Confirm and Verify**
    - Click on **Add Domain** to submit your domain.
    - Follow the verification process to complete the setup.

Once verified, your widget will be accessible through your custom domain.

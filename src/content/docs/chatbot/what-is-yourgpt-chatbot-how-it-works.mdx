YourGPT is an **AI-First Platform** that lets you build intelligent **AI Agents and Chatbots**—without writing any code.  

It helps businesses automate conversations across customer support, sales, onboarding, and internal operations.  

Fully customisable, multilingual (100+ languages), and built to handle any use case or industry.

## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>How it works?</div>
Our AI solution harnesses the power of most advanced State-of-the-Art (SOTA) AI models and cutting-edge techniques to provide users with precise, contextually relevant responses to their inquiries.

YourGPT lets you train AI using your own content—text, PDF files, Notion, Google Drive, Dropbox, Confluence, YouTube, and more.  
It includes a self-learning AI that gets better over time through real interactions.

For advanced workflows, YourGPT provides AI Studio—a powerful tool to design guided, step-by-step agents with full control over logic and performance.  
Whether you're scaling support or building task-specific agents, YourGPT gives you everything you need—fast, flexible, and reliable.

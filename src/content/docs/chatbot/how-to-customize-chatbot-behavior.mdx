 🗣️ Change Behavior of your Chatbot
</Note>

To customise the behavior and responses of your chatbot, follow the steps below:

### Step 1: Go to General Settings

Start by navigating to the **General Settings** of your chatbot. This is usually found in the main dashboard or settings panel of your chatbot platform.

### Step 2: Go to Model

Once you are in the General Settings, find and click on the "**Model**" section. This is where you can adjust the underlying model that your chatbot uses to process and respond to user inputs.

### Step 3: Change Base Prompt

In the Model section, look for the "**Base Prompt**" setting. This is the initial prompt that your chatbot uses to start conversations. Changing this will influence how your model behaves.


<Image
  src="/assets/chatbot/PromptControl.png"
  alt="Changing the Base Prompt"
/>

For example, if your base prompt is very formal, your chatbot will tend to respond in a formal manner. If it's more casual, your chatbot will respond in kind.

### Step 4: Changing the Model

Changing the model can also help in achieving better responses. While GPT-4 responds very well to complex queries, it is more expensive. Therefore, for general queries, GPT-3.5-turbo is a better choice. It provides a good balance between performance and cost.

---
Remember to save your changes before exiting the settings panel. Your chatbot's behavior should now be updated according to the new Changes.

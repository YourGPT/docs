<div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'> Sharing Your Chatbot Link</div>

To share or test your custom chatbot using a link, follow the steps below:

<Aside >🔗 Share your custom chatbot using a link</Aside>

<Steps>
1. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Go to YourGPT Chatbot Integration</div>

     Start by logging into your YourGPT dashboard and navigate to the "Integration" section. You can find this in the left sidebar menu after selecting your chatbot.

2. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Copy the Link</div>

    At the top of the integration page, locate the sharing options toolbar containing: "**Link**", "**Script**", "**Iframe**", "**WidgetUID**", and "**QR**". 
    
    Select the "**Link**" option to reveal your chatbot's shareable URL. Click the copy button or manually select and copy this URL to share your custom chatbot with others.

      <div className="flex justify-center items-center bg-blue-500 rounded-md p-4">
          <Image src="/assets/chatbot/link-share.png" alt="Options to Shareable Chatbot"     
          width="100%"
          height="100%"
          style={{ width: "100%" }}
          className="rounded-md"/>
      </div>

3. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Share the Link</div>

     Share the copied URL with anyone you want to have access to your chatbot. They can use this link to interact with your chatbot directly.

</Steps>

 <Aside type="danger">Remember, anyone with the link can access your chatbot, so be careful who you share it with.</Aside>

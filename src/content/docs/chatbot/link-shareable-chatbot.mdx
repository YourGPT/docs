## <div className='tracking-tight font-bold'> Sharing Your Chatbot Link</div>
To share your custom chatbot using a link, follow the steps below:

<Steps>
1. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Go to YourGPT Chatbot Integration</div>

     Start by navigating to the YourGPT Chatbot Integration. This is usually found in the main dashboard or settings panel of your chatbot platform.
   

2. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Copy the LinkStep </div>

    At the top of the screen, you will see several options: "**Script**", "**Iframe**", and "**Link**". Click on the "**Link**" option. This will reveal a URL that is the direct link to your custom chatbot. Copy this URL.

    <Image src={linkshare} alt="Link Shareable Chatbot"/>

3. <div className='font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl'>Share the LinkStep</div>

     Share the copied URL with anyone you want to have access to your chatbot. They can use this link to interact with your chatbot directly.

     
    <Note type="re" color="rose"> Remember, anyone with the link can access your chatbot, so be careful who you share it with. </Note>
</Steps>

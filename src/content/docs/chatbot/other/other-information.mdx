## Roles & Privileges


|Roles|Abilities|
|---|---|
|Owner|Can view, update, add, and delete various elements; Has the privileges to create invitations.|
|Editor|Granted permissions to view, edit, and update content; Does not have privileges to manage invitations.|
|Viewer|Limited to viewing the project only; Can access the conversation tab.|
|Operator|Can view and manage conversations; Does not have access to the project settings.|

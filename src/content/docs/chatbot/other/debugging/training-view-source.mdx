Training a chatbot involves using machine learning algorithms to analyze user interactions and improve responses over time. By employing natural language processing (NLP), the chatbot learns to understand context and user intent. Continuous feedback and updates refine its capabilities, enabling it to provide more accurate, relevant answers, making it effective for applications such as customer support and personal assistance.

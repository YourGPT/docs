Follow these guidelines to train your YourGPT chatbot effectively and ensure it provides the best possible user experience.


### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Best Practices for Training</div> 


|Practice|Description|
|---|---|
|Consistency|Use a consistent format for all training data.|
|Clarity|Ensure questions and answers are clear and to the point.|
|Comprehensiveness|Cover as many potential user queries as possible in your training data. The more the chatbot is trained on, the better it will be at handling a wide range of user queries.|
|Accuracy|Ensure that the training data is accurate and up to date.|
|Periodic Updates|Periodically update the training data to reflect new information.|
|Test Frequently|Regularly test the chatbot to ensure it's providing accurate responses.|


## <div className='text-3xl tracking-tight font-bold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>YourGPT Training Options:</div>

- **Links:** Train your chatbot by using relevant links.
- **Documents:** Implement the training process effectively using documentation.
- **FAQs:** Train the chatbot using frequently asked questions.
- **Text:** Provide concise and clear text for quick comprehension and implementation.
- **Third party:** Use platforms such as Notion, Google Docs, YouTube, and more for a variety of training options.

### <div className='text-2xl tracking-tight font-semibold dark:text-inherit text-gray-700'>Training with FAQs</div>
FAQs are an excellent method for providing the chatbot with frequently asked questions and their corresponding answers. This helps the chatbot to promptly address user queries.

- Identify the most common questions from your users.
- Provide clear and concise answers to each question.

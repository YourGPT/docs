# Documents

> Train your AI chatbot using document files like DOC, CSV, PDF, PPT and more

## Quick Start

Train your bot easily by adding documents like DOC, CSV, PDF, PPT and more.

  1. Go to the **Training** section in YourGPT Dashboard

  2. Click **Add New**
    ![Add New](/assets/chatbot/training/image-10.png)

  3. A window will pop up to add your documents
    ![Add Documents](/assets/chatbot/training/image-11.png)

  4. Add your document and start training
    ![Add Document](/assets/chatbot/training/image-12.png)

## Check Training Source

To see how your chatbot is being trained from a document, follow these steps:

  1. Click on the document search icon
    ![Document Search](/assets/chatbot/training/image-13.png)

  2. You will be redirected to debug lab where you can see the document source
    ![Debug Lab](/assets/chatbot/training/image-14.png)

> **INFO:** For more information on searching, making changes, and removing chunks from trained documents, check out the [Training View Source documentation](/chatbot/debugging/conversation-logs/training-audit#view-training-source).

## Delete Document

To remove a document from your training data:

  1. Click on the remove document icon
    ![Remove Document](/assets/chatbot/training/image-15.png)

  2. Confirm the deletion
    ![Confirm Deletion](/assets/chatbot/training/image-16.png)

> **INFO:** For detailed step-by-step instructions, visit our help article on [How to Train Chatbot using Documents Files](https://help.yourgpt.ai/article/how-to-train-chatbot-using-documents-files-56).

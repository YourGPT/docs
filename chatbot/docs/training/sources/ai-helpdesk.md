# AI Helpdesk

> Create and manage Help Desk articles, including AI-generated drafts

## Quick Start

> **INFO:** Articles created in **AI Helpdesk** are ingested as a chatbot **training source**. When you add or update an article, YourGPT processes the content into searchable chunks and indexes them for retrieval. During conversations, the chatbot can fetch these chunks as grounding context to generate accurate answers.

## Create an Article

  1. In your YourGPT dashboard, open **AI Helpdesk** from the left sidebar.

  2. Click **Add New** to create a new article.
    ![Add New](/assets/chatbot/training/image-21.png)

  3. In the editor, click **Create Article with AI**.
    ![Create Article with AI](/assets/chatbot/training/image-22.png)
    > **INFO:** You can also create an article manually by filling in the **title**, **small description**, and **article content** in the editor. For the fastest workflow, we recommend using **Create Article with AI** and then editing the draft.

  4. Add your prompt (and optionally attach **Images**, **Voice Notes**, or **URLs**), then click **Generate**.
    ![AI Article Prompt](/assets/chatbot/training/image-23.png)

  5. Review the **Generated Article Preview**
    - Use **Regenerate** to try again
    - Use **Edit in Editor** to make changes before adding
    ![Generated Article Preview](/assets/chatbot/training/image-24.png)

  6. When you're ready, click **Add** to save/add the article.
    ![Add Article](/assets/chatbot/training/image-25.png)

## Import Articles

If you already have articles prepared, you can import them into AI Helpdesk:

  1. Open **AI Helpdesk** and click **Import Articles**.
    ![Import Articles](/assets/chatbot/training/image-26.png)

# Webflow

> Learn how to integrate your GPT AI chatbot into your Webflow project using the official Webflow app.

Integrate YourGPT Chatbot into your Webflow website to engage visitors, answer questions, and capture leads automatically. Our official Webflow App makes the integration process seamless and code-free.

<Callout type="tip" title="Webflow App Advantage">
  Using the official Webflow App is the recommended method. It automatically handles the script placement and project permissions, ensuring a stable and secure connection.
</Callout>

---

## Installation Guide

Follow these steps to connect your chatbot to your Webflow project via the official Marketplace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Webflow Marketplace">
    Log in to your Webflow dashboard and visit the <a href="https://webflow.com/apps/detail/yourgpt-chatbot" target="_blank" className="no-underline font-bold">YourGPT Chatbot App</a> in the marketplace. Click **Install**.

      ![Install YourGPT Chatbot](/assets/chatbot/webflow/image1.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your Project">
    Choose the specific Webflow project where you want to add the chatbot and proceed to the next step.

      ![Select Webflow Project](/assets/chatbot/webflow/image2.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize Integration">
    Review the permissions and click **Authorize** to grant the app access to your selected project.

      ![Authorize App](/assets/chatbot/webflow/image4.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure and Go Live">
    Once authorized, the chatbot is automatically added to your project. You can now visit your site to see the AI agent in action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Success!">
  Your AI chatbot is now integrated and live on your Webflow website.
</Callout>

## Advanced Options

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Manual Code Integration">
    If you prefer to add the chatbot manually using a code snippet:
    1. Go to your [YourGPT Dashboard](https://chatbot.yourgpt.ai/dashboard/).
    2. Navigate to **Integrations → Website Builders → Custom Website**.
    3. Copy the providing script.
    4. In Webflow, go to **Project Settings → Custom Code**.
    5. Paste the script into the **Footer Code** section and click **Save**.
    6. **Publish** your site to see the changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Troubleshooting Tips">
    - **Not showing up?** Ensure you have published your Webflow site after installing the app.
    - **Z-index issues?** If the chatbot is hidden behind other elements, you can adjust the widget's Z-index in the **Widget Settings** on the YourGPT Dashboard.
    - **Domain restriction?** Make sure your domain is whitelisted in your chatbot's security settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Carrd

> Guide to integrating the YourGPT Chatbot with your Carrd website

> **TIP:** ## Installation
> 
> Integrating YourGPT Chatbot with Carrd is a simple process that ensures seamless interaction with your website. Follow these steps:
> 
> <ol>
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Sign in to Carrd:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>Log in to your Carrd account.</li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Access Site Editor:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>Open the site editor for the specific Carrd website where you want to integrate the chatbot.</li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Add an Element:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>
>         Click on the <strong><code>Add an element</code></strong> (Plus icon) for your website.
>       </li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Select Embed:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>
>         Scroll down and choose the <strong><code>Embed</code></strong> option within the{" "}
>         <strong><code>Add an element</code></strong> section. This allows you to add custom scripts or HTML code.
>       </li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">YourGPT Chatbot Integration:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>
>         Visit the YourGPT Chatbot integration page. Copy the <strong><code>code snippet</code></strong> from the
>         integrations section at the top.
>       </li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Paste the Code:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>
>         Return to the Carrd website editor and paste the copied Chatbot code into the embed area meant for custom
>         scripts or HTML.
>       </li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> 
>   <li>
>     <div className="font-bold dark:text-gray-300 text-xl">Save Changes:</div>
>     <ul>
>       <li>Ensure to save the modifications made in the <strong><code>Custom Code</code></strong> section within Carrd.</li>
>     </ul>
>   </li>
> </ol>
> 
> Congratulations! YourGPT Chatbot is now successfully integrated into your Carrd website.

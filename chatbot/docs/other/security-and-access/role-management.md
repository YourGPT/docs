# Roles & Privileges

> Organization-level and project-level roles with billing, access, and permission details.

Roles define what each user can view or manage across your organization and individual projects.  
Permissions are separated into organization-level access and project-level access to keep billing, settings, and daily work clearly controlled.

---

## Organization Roles & Privileges

Organization roles control billing access and organization-wide settings.

| Role | Billing Access | Organization Access |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Owner** | Full billing access, subscription management, plan and usage visibility | Full organization control, member management, role changes, invitation control, organization deletion |
| **Editor** | Subscription management and plan visibility | Update and view organization details, view members |
| **Viewer** | Plan visibility only | View organization details and member list |
| **Billing** | Full billing access and subscription management | No access to organization settings or members |

---

## Project Roles & Privileges

Project roles define what users can do inside a specific project.

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full project access, resource management, project invitations |
| **Editor** | View and edit project content, update configurations |
| **Viewer** | Read-only project access, conversation visibility |
| **Operator** | Conversation management without project settings access |
| **Trainer** | Chatbot training and dataset management |

---

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
  Roles are assigned independently at the organization and project level.
</Callout>

Billing access is always controlled at the organization level and cannot be granted through project roles.
